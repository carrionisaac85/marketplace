## Brand
- App: WantBoard — reverse marketplace (buyers post wants, sellers find them)
- Site: https://www.want-board.com
- Colors: #E84B2A (accent), #1A1A1A (dark), #F7F5F2 (off-white), #2ECC71 (green/budget)
- Fonts: Syne 800 (headlines) + DM Sans 400/700 (body) — both Google Fonts
- Logo: extract inline SVG from www.want-board.com homepage

## Campaign
- Objective: App installs (direct-response)
- Platforms: Instagram Feed 1080×1080 + 1080×1350 portrait; Stories/Reels 1080×1920

## 5 Angles

| # | Angle | Headline | Benefit | CTA | BG color |
|---|-------|----------|---------|-----|----------|
| 1 | Pain point (buyers) | "Stop scrolling. Start asking." | Post what you want. Sellers come to you. | Get the App | #1A1A1A |

| 5 | Urgency/Social | "Post once. Get offers today." | Real buyers, real budgets, near you. | Post Your Want | #2ECC71 |

## Ad structure (golden rules)
- 4 elements only: Logo (top-left) + Headline + Benefit + CTA button
- Under 20 words on image, flat solid bg (no gradients), left-align everything
- Files go in artifacts/mockup-sandbox/public/ads/
- Canvas: 600×600 iframes in a row; 450×600 portrait stacked below each

## To build
Switch to Build mode and say "build the ads" — launch 5 parallel design subagents, one per angle, square + portrait variants each.
